# Index of files read (summary per file; full content in notes/<path>.md)

- src/actionsWiring.ts: The dock's Actions: the pistol (shootAt, shootVehicle), the bomb (strikeAt, explodeAt) and the fires (ignite, tick). All work runs on a player's click or on the fire step (0.5 s of sim time), never per frame for nothing.
- src/bayPool.ts: The lots' parking bays worked out on job workers (`bays.worker.ts`): asks split in contiguous shares, one per lane, answers joined in order.
- src/bays.worker.ts: The bays service of the job workers: answers a share of lots with baysOfShare, beating every 500 ms while it works.
- src/buildingsWiring.ts: The Builder's composition: the building tool, its workspace panels, the 3D reference model, the player's blueprints and their photos, the inspector fields, the overlay, and the per-frame hand-off (beforeDraw) of the cutaway and the preview to the renderer.
- src/cityGen.worker.ts: The city generator served on a job worker: either the whole city grown into tables (buildCityTableOf) or only laid out with each tile's growth package (layoutCityOf + tilePackagesOf).
- src/cityGrowth.worker.ts: A city's tiles grown on the job workers, many at once: 'grow' one tile's lots (growTile), or 'assemble' the tiles' tables into the city's buildings and lots tables.
- src/conflictPool.ts: The traffic's conflict pairs, junction turns, sweeps, finished pairs, whole builds' junctions and walkway corners measured on the job workers (up to four lanes), each ask split in contiguous shares with only the data its share reads.
- src/conflictTable.worker.ts: The conflict service of the job workers: answers 'turns', 'junctions', 'corners', 'finish', 'sweeps' and 'pairs' asks from conflictPool, beating every 500 ms; the pairs' tables are kept on disk for the next session before the answer goes back.
- src/core/aabb.ts: A 2D axis-aligned box type, built from a flat xy buffer, and grown by a margin.
- src/core/arrayPack.ts: Typed arrays and numbers packed in one buffer (u32 head length, JSON head, arrays 8-byte aligned), read back as views with no copy.
- src/core/assert.ts: The session's problem log: `ensure(condition, message)` returns the condition and reports a failure once per distinct message (console and a reporter).
- src/core/axisCut.ts: Cuts a polygon with holes along an axis-parallel line into the parts below and above, by walking each ring's crossings and joining the chains along the cut; null where the cut cannot be made cleanly.
- src/core/bezier.ts: Quadratic Bezier road curves stored as a shape (t along the chord, h off it): control point from shape and back, evaluation, flattening within a tolerance (1-64 steps), de Casteljau split, tightest radius in closed form, and shape fitted to a minimum radius by bisection.
- src/core/changeLog.ts: A log of changes between opaque state tokens: a reader holds a token and asks `since(token)` for the changes after it (null: read everything again). States no reader holds (collected) are merged away when the log doubles.
- src/core/clipper.ts: Polygon boolean operations (union, difference, intersection) on a shared integer grid (25,000 steps a metre): Clipper2's integer kernel first, the Martinez kernel (polygon-clipping) when Clipper2 throws, and Clipper2 again when Martinez fails first; area of a multipolygon.
- src/core/commands.ts: The command registry: named commands with an optional enabled test, and key rules (by physical code and/or character, modifiers, a when test) resolved last-bound first.
- src/core/derivedCache.ts: The derived-data cache in IndexedDB ('roadcraft-derived' v2): a general store plus own stores for cellWork, roadtiles, ecology and farWoods; reads (one, keys, some, all), writes (fire-and-forget, now, durable, queued under a byte budget, many, many-committed), forgets, and a byte estimate of a value.
- src/core/digest.ts: An exact two-lane digest of a stream of numbers, strings and plain values (FNV-like and murmur-like lanes mixed per 32-bit word), giving an integer under 2^53; used to key derived data by content.
- src/core/dynamicResolution.ts: Dynamic resolution (an option, off by default): every 30 GPU frame costs, the 90th percentile picks a scale among fixed steps (1 .. floor) so the frame aims at 85% of the budget; falls at once, rises one step at a time, ignores 5 samples after a change.
- src/core/engine.ts: The products engine: registered products refreshed per phase ('frame', 'draw', 'step') in dependency order, each refresh timed and counted in a ledger; a watched stamp tells when a product computed with no input moved ("idle").
- src/core/events.ts: The game's input queue: browser events pushed (each push wakes a frame), drained once a frame in order; what a handler pushes waits for the next frame.
- src/core/fillet.ts: Corner rounding for road junction kerbs: the fillet arc between two leaving directions (tangent points, centre, run), the acute setback where two widened rays stop overlapping, and the kerb return radius between two half-widths (2.8-7.2 m).
- src/core/forkMap.ts: Copy-on-write Map and Set over another (a document's working copy for an edit): reads fall through to the map below, writes cost only what they change; changes() and applyTo write the result back.
- src/core/gameState.ts: The game's state (what the player has in hand, how the game runs): typed values with a ring of the last 512 changes; watchers told the changes of their keys once per flush (a microtask after a set, and each frame).
- src/core/gpuTier.ts: The graphics tier a machine starts on, by the renderer string (WEBGL_debug_renderer_info): software/phones low, NVIDIA RTX x060 and up ultra, AMD RX x700+ ultra, Intel integrated low, Apple M ultra/high; unknown medium.
- src/core/health.ts: The game's health log (a ring of 256 problems, repeats counted) and the per-frame system timer (each frame's milliseconds per system, kept 30 s or 4096 frames), with percentile statistics.
- src/core/heap.ts: The game's one binary min-heap of numbers by priority, in three typed columns (item, key, arrival order), ties broken by an optional `before` then by arrival.
- src/core/hitch.ts: measureHitch: a `performance.measure` entry for the frame monitor and profiler, in a development build only.
- src/core/hypot.ts: hypot2 and hypot3: Math.hypot to the bit in plain JS (scaled by the largest, Kahan-summed squares), so V8 inlines them and boxes no number per call.
- src/core/identityMemo.ts: identityMemo: a value computed once per key object (WeakMap, freed with the key); uniqueCounter: a counter of numbers never handed out twice in the page.
- src/core/idSlots.ts: Dense slots for sparse numeric ids on typed arrays: an open-addressing table (linear probing, tombstones, power-of-two size, load under 0.6) mapping each id to a slot of the caller's columns; freed slots reused; `grown` widens a column.
- src/core/intersect.ts: 2D intersections: line-line (parametric), segment-segment (t, u in [0,1]), closest point on a segment, and a forward slab test.
- src/core/keptList.ts: A list kept in localStorage under one key (blueprints, road templates): read entries (unreadable ones kept as they are), readShared (the same list while the stored text is unchanged), update (an unreadable list set aside first), all with failures said.
- src/core/minMax.ts: least and most: Math.min and Math.max of two numbers to the bit (NaN and signed zero kept) as comparisons the optimiser keeps unboxed.
- src/core/offset.ts: Offsets an open polyline by a signed distance (miter joins, round joins past the miter limit, half-circle caps on reversals) and prunes the loops the inner side makes.
- src/core/perfBudget.ts: The performance budgets table: per-step ms budgets (warn), per-view triangle/draw-call/GPU/fps budgets, GPU memory per group, textures, targets, heap; memoryGroup classifies a mesh by name; cityBudgetTable compares a measure with the budgets.
- src/core/plain.ts: Deep equality of plain values: samePlain (an undefined key is no key; NaN equals NaN) and sameText (the same JSON text, keys in the same order, without writing it).
- src/core/polygon.ts: Polygon basics: isSimple (pairs of edges tested by box first, segSeg only where boxes meet), signed area, ray-cast point in polygon, monotone-chain convex hull.
- src/core/polygonBands.ts: A ring's edges binned into horizontal bands (Haines' bins) so a point-in-polygon test reads only the edges of the point's band; built by a counting sort.
- src/core/polyline.ts: An immutable polyline on a flat Float64Array with lazily built tables (arc lengths, unit tangents, bbox, chunk and group boxes for nearest-point search); sampling by arc length (allocating or into a reused frame), sub-polylines, reversal, nearest point.
- src/core/profiler.ts: The surgical profiler: per-frame counts of disposals, builds and uploads with their call sites (stack traces), engine products' timings, diary changes, heap readings, long tasks and frame steps, rolled into a one-second window for the HUD. Its comment says it is closed by default, but `on` starts TRUE.
- src/core/recentCache.ts: A bounded LRU cache on a Map's insertion order: a hit is moved to the newest, a miss made and held, the oldest dropped past capacity.
- src/core/recordMap.ts: A Map subclass that tells a listener each key's value before a set, delete or clear (an edit being recorded for undo).
- src/core/regionIndex.ts: Point location, boundary distance, digests and line spans over a set of rings in constant time per query: the rings clustered, each cluster a dense grid of cells (default 8 units) holding the segments its supercover touches plus a reference winding number at every cell centre.
- src/core/rerootMap.ts: A persistent map by rerooting (Baker's shallow binding): the root holds the values, every other version holds its differences from the one it points to; deriving a new version reroots to the current one first, so a new version costs what it writes.
- src/core/ring.ts: A closed boundary of lines and exact arcs kept as one plain array of numbers (area and box cached in it); edges, vertices and flattened points made on each call.
- src/core/rng.ts: The deterministic RNG (xoshiro128** seeded by splitmix32): u32, float, range, int, bool, pick, Box-Muller normal, weighted pick, fork by tag, state/fromState; plus mulberry32 as a closure generator.
- src/core/runPool.ts: Runs of numbers by integer key in one shared Float64Array (start and length per key in Int32 columns); a dropped run stays until a put overflows, then the live runs are packed (same size while a quarter stays free, else 1.5x what is needed).
- src/core/sampleRing.ts: A fixed ring of the last `capacity` samples (Float32), no allocation per push; nearest-rank percentile stats on demand; STUTTER_MS (two periods at 59 Hz).
- src/core/scalar.ts: The engine's numeric tolerances and tiny scalar helpers: EPS family, GEO_EPS 0.04 mm, SIN_EPS (3 deg), ACUTE_EPS (12 deg), MITER limits, FLATTEN_TOL 2 cm, ARC_TOL 8 mm, ribbons and seams; steadyRatio, clamp, hypot2 (plain sqrt), lerp, normalizeAngle.
- src/core/simplifyLine.ts: Ramer-Douglas-Peucker simplification of an open line within `tol`, iterative with an explicit stack and a keep mask.
- src/core/statechart.ts: A statechart with SCXML semantics for the game's tools and modes: hierarchical states, queued events processed to completion, transitions with guards, actions and targets; exit up to the domain, actions, entry down to the target; the last 256 transitions recorded.
- src/core/units.ts: The one unit system: 1 world unit = 1 metre; m(), perM(), TUNED_UNIT (0.4 m, the old unit), stepsIn (steps of a length read to a millionth), kmh (km/h to units per second).
- src/core/vec2.ts: 2D vector helpers on immutable {x, y} objects: add, sub, scale, neg, dot, cross, lengths, perp, normalize, lerp, angle, fromAngle, addScaled.
- src/core/weakMachine.ts: A weaker machine played on this one for a run (build-time ROADCRAFT_WEAK): threads, GPU name, memory GB and pixel ratio overrides; the preset uhd770 (4 threads, Intel UHD 770, 8 GB, dpr 1).
- src/core/weightedSlots.ts: Ordered slots each empty or of one of a few classes, with one Fenwick tree of counts per class, so a weighted draw over thousands of slots (and the k-th occupied slot) is found in log steps.
- src/core/workerLike.ts: WorkerLike: the shape of a worker its owner uses (a page Worker, or a worker in another process over a port).
- src/desktopJobs.ts: On the desktop shell, the long-running job workers are threads of utility processes (desktop/jobs.cjs): each new long worker asks the shell for a port and wraps it in a ProcessWorker; a lost process fails its workers; desktopJobThreads reads how many job threads the shell runs.
- src/desktopSaves.ts: The desktop's saves: the autosave's stores as files in the Steam Auto-Cloud folder, kept by the shell's saves utility process over a MessagePort (get, write, batch, keys, marks); an older IndexedDB map copied once into an empty folder.
- src/desktopShown.ts: Whether the desktop window is minimized or hidden, as the shell tells it by a window message (the shell disables background throttling, so document.hidden never says it).
- src/desktopSim.ts: On the desktop, the simulation's worker is a thread of a utility process (its own V8 heap), reached over a port the shell hands in order; a lost process fails the worker.
- src/editor/barriers.ts: The walls tool: a wall, fence or hedge traced point by point, each point snapped behind the kerb or along the footway's back of the nearest road (ribbons near the point only), checked not to reach a road, built in one undo step.
- src/editor/blocks.ts: A grid of streets laid in one gesture: the grid's lines (rotated about a centre) and each committed as a road path whose ends join what is already there.
- src/editor/blueprintLibrary.ts: The player's saved building designs (blueprints) in localStorage through KeptList: list, save by name, a photo (PNG data URL) kept INSIDE each entry, photoOf, remove; each stored body rescaled and repaired on read.
- src/editor/buildingFacade.ts: A barrel re-exporting the facade patterns from the building model.
- src/editor/buildingFloors.ts: Each building's floor height, worked out once and kept; the floors the document's changes reach (buildings by id, terrain/roads/elevation by rectangle grown by FLOOR_READ_REACH) forgotten through a journal cursor.
- src/editor/buildingPlans.ts: Building plan editing: starter shapes and primitives, a volume's ring stored in its bounding frame, vertex/edge moves, bevels, offsets (Clipper2 inflate), vertex insert/remove with facade sides remapped, masses added at ground or on a roof (shrunk by bisection to its support).
- src/editor/buildingProfile.ts: Profile edits of a building: a mass split at a floor (reliefs split between the two), and a tier reshaped to another plan with facade work carried by face direction.
- src/editor/buildingRoofs.ts: Roof details of a mass (solar, skylight, vent, chimney, water tank, spire, lantern): add at a point if it fits, update with checks, remove.
- src/editor/buildings.ts: The building commands and operations: storeys, resizing, wings, setbacks, removal with support, roofs, parameters, level heights, facade components and zones, rotation and scaling, place/add/edit/replace/delete/duplicate, clearing buildings the roads reach, grouping, element fusing, block moves and snapping, union, welding neighbours, clipping, reliefs, roof shape, free elements, mirroring, repeats.
- src/editor/buildingSnap.ts: Where a footprint goes under the pointer: onto the back of the nearest road's footway (grid-stepped along it, slid flush at a corner), else aligned to the nearest building's bearing, else to the grid; then made flush with neighbours of the same bearing. Runs once per pointer move, by place only.
- src/editor/buildingTool.ts: (written as the file is read) The building tool's state machine in world coordinates: placing blueprints and drawn shapes, editing volumes, faces, roofs, elements, interiors; previews validated as the pointer moves.
- src/editor/bulldozer.ts: The bulldozer: a click removes the one thing under the pointer (building, pole, street item, road), a dragged box removes everything inside it in one undo step; roads removed with their street kit and a share of their price back.
- src/editor/chunkedSave.ts: The map saved by squares (manifest 8): each square of each keyed layer a binary frame cut from the map's tables (rows copied, nothing decoded); the ordered lists by squares too; the globals apart; a save writes only the squares the diary says changed. Reads every older format (7, 6, 5 JSON tiles, 3 parts) and writes it whole as 8 on the next save.
- src/editor/cityGenerator.ts: The city generator's hand on the map: a plan's street graph laid as roads, footways widened to 3 m (avenues given a planted median), squares in each neighbourhood, power lines, furniture and street names for the whole city, every lot zoned by its district.
- src/editor/cityInTiles.ts: A new city made on many job workers: laid out by one (cityGen 'layout'), its tiles' lots grown four phases one after another (each tile given its already-grown neighbours' edge buffers), then the tiles' tables joined by one 'assemble' job.
- src/editor/cityTiles.ts: A city's buildings grown tile by tile on many workers: the laid-out city cut into 1 km tile packages (lots, roads within 150 m), each tile grown on its own small document reading its earlier neighbours' edge buildings, buildings in a tile-own id range and spilled to a table as they grow; the tiles' tables joined into the city's by copying rows.
- src/editor/cloudTool.ts: The cloud tool (Landscape > Terrain > Clouds): add a cloud under the pointer at the brush's height, pick the repeat seen under the pointer, remove/edit/move it (the wind's drift taken off what is kept).
- src/editor/commit.ts: (written as the file is read) Committing roads: a gesture's pieces laid on a forked copy of the document and network, judged (edit rules, funds), deep cuts bored as tunnels, and taken back into the live map as one atomic edit; dry runs kept so the click lays the copy already judged.
- src/editor/cookedCities.ts: The cities shipped with the game (the first new city of each size, seed 7, grown at build time): read from gzip files when this build's generator fingerprint matches, otherwise null (grown here).
- src/editor/editRules.ts: What a road edit may not leave behind, judged on the working copy of the network: sharper junctions, squeezed legs, ramps steeper than Baldwin Street, a carriageway on another; guardRoadEdit runs an in-place edit on a fork, judges and pays it.
- src/editor/editStream.ts: A seeded random stream for an edit of the document, from the diary's version and the place, so an edit can be made again the same.
- src/editor/fromReference.ts: Builds a building blueprint body from a reference 3D model (its triangles and a colour sampler): massing blocks from the upward faces, wall and plinth colours sampled per storey, each facade bay classed by how dark it shows, the roof colour, and a lantern crown when the model rises above the highest block.
- src/editor/gameKeys.ts: Every key the game answers, written as commands and ordered key rules of the core command registry; main.ts hands in the game's actions (tools, camera, save/open, undo, terrain brush, monitors).
- src/editor/growthFeed.ts: The page's side of the city's growth: picks the next lot, sends the growth worker only what it reads round the lot (buildings as shared-table row refs, roads and junction kerb rings, the region's ground and paving) and keeps the worker's replica in step from the buildings' diary and the network's changes; writes the buildings that come back after checking them again when the document moved.
- src/editor/growthReplica.ts: The growth worker's side: a replica of what the growth reads (neighbour buildings decoded from shared tables, roads and kerb rings filed in cells, the lot's ground and paving), fed incrementally by the page; each ask grows the first lot and then the nearest open lots of the region (up to 32), a read outside what was sent being a miss the page answers by resending with a margin.
- src/editor/history.ts: Undo/redo by the document's patches (records an edit changed), with grouped steps undone together, a step and byte budget (60 steps, 96 MiB), and the map loaders: restoreInto (a saved or generated city replacing the document whole, network rebuilt, repairs skipped for a generated city) and restoreSnapshot.
- src/editor/lotTool.ts: The zoning tool: lots drawn, edited and zoned by the player, the proposed street lots planned off the page, and the lot overlay (one group per lot within a window of 250 m cells round the ground the view sees, the proposal's lots, the gesture) handed to the scene by changed groups.
- src/editor/mapExport.ts: Save map: the map file as one Blob of framed pieces (header, settings JSON, the document's binary sections), offered as a download through an object URL revoked ten seconds later.
- src/editor/nodeMover.ts: Dragging a road node: the node follows the pointer live in the document, the network preview rebuilt no sooner than its own last cost allows; on release the node goes back and the move is replayed as one checked edit (moveNodeChecked) carrying the street kit.
- src/editor/offPageNetwork.ts: The network's jobs asked of the job pool: a legacy map's repairs over the whole serialized document (networkBake), a tile's junctions and ribbons as the view reaches it (roadBake), and the tiles a map opens with round its centre.
- src/editor/persistence.ts: Autosave and loading: the map saved by squares into IndexedDB (TiledSaves, encoded in the job system's save lane) when available, else as one text in localStorage or the large store; debounced (700 ms, at most 3 s) and flushed in idle time; settings apart; unreadable copies set aside, never deleted; map files read (binary frame or older JSON); strict validation of a serialized document.
- src/editor/poleRoutes.ts: The pole lines' route graph (stations every 2 m along the footway contours, spans across the streets of a region) in typed arrays, and Dijkstra over it with a heap, integer micrometre costs and ties broken by place, reusing scratch arrays wiped only where a run reached.
- src/editor/poles.ts: The pole tool and the planning of a pole run: ends snapped onto standing poles or the footway contours, the route searched over the pole lines of a region round the run that doubles until the route is surely shortest, corners kept by Douglas-Peucker, poles spaced evenly off crossing landings, standing poles reused; a batch planner by 1 km tiles.
- src/editor/preparedCity.ts: A whole city made off the page kept as record tables (buildings, landscape, lots, poles, spans), never as posted objects: prepareCity turns a serialized city's records into tables in batches of 2,000 (members shared per batch, not interned), and the helpers that hand the page an opened city's tables.
- src/editor/regionFrames.ts: The binary frames of a map saved by regions: a square's records (a record table cut from the map's, or JSON) with their order places, a square's wears as id/decay pairs, and small values (globals, the manifest) as JSON frames; plus the codec that reads them back from stored blobs.
- src/editor/repair.ts: Repairs of a legacy map's dead ends that visibly end inside another road: found (nearest candidate over every dead end, roads near it), split and merged, up to 64; or replayed on the page from steps found off the page.
- src/editor/roadParking.ts: The road tool's parking preset (none, parallel both/right/left, cycle, cycle and parking), kept in localStorage, and the segment parking a new road is drawn with.
- src/editor/roadPath.ts: A freehand road gesture turned into a chain of tangent-continuous quadratic pieces: samples thinned to 7.2 m, simplified (plan and height errors) with a growing tolerance until at most 48 controls, pieces joined at the midpoints between controls.
- src/editor/roads/economy.ts: Paying for a road edit: the roads' value and the balance before the edit (roadsBefore), and the charge for the value after against it, written into the edited document when it can be paid (settleRoadEdit).
- src/editor/roads/powerLine.ts: The power line of new streets: for each street with a footway (not tunnelled, its open stretch above ground) a pole run is planned along its left footway between its mouths, consecutive runs joined at two-road nodes; many streets planned over shared 1 km tiles; a generator yields every 6 streets.
- src/editor/roads/profile.ts: A road profile applied to roads already built ("apply without demolishing"): each segment's type, direction, lanes, section and parking set from the profile, stopping at the first segment whose type the profile cannot fit; whether anything changed.
- src/editor/roads/streetKit.ts: A street's power line and furniture carried through an edit of its road: what stands on the edited streets' footways (nearer to them than to any other street) is found before the edit, removed after it, and laid again on the roads the edit left (or removed with them).
- src/editor/roadTool.ts: The road tool: drags, chains and curves drawn, snapped to roads and the grid, at a chosen height; the draft judged by a dry run 120 ms after it rests (verdict, cost, stations) and judged again whenever the map near it (or the balance) changes; the road laid in one undo step with its profile/width, cut walls, furniture, power line and name.
- src/editor/roadTop.ts: The highest road node's solved height, kept per world cell from the diary's roads and elevation entries (a Derived product): only the cells an entry's rectangles reach are recomputed; a histogram of whole heights gives the top.
- src/editor/roundabout.ts: A single-lane roundabout placed on vacant ground: refused when its size, the map's bounds or roads near it forbid; else eight ring nodes (priority on the even ones), eight one-way curved ring segments and four approaches, laid on a fork, judged by the forked network, taken back.
- src/editor/save.worker.ts: The autosave's writer, a job-system service: each batch of changed binary frames (transferred from the page) compressed and written to IndexedDB one after another, answered by id; failures logged and answered false.
- src/editor/saveBinary.ts: The save's binary frames: a magic word, a format, a directory of sections (kind, offset, length) and 8-byte aligned sections; packed as pieces (for a Blob) or one buffer (transferable); read back as views without copying; JSON and Float64 helpers; gzip through Compression Streams, kept plain where the browser lacks them.
- src/editor/saveLayers.ts: The layers of the map saved by squares: each keyed kind (where a record stands, how its square's table is cut, its saved form, its index by place), each ordered list (items by reference with a fractional order and a square, held in two WeakMaps per list), the global parts, and the square helpers.
- src/editor/saveStore.ts: The saves' IndexedDB store and its batches: PartStore (get, write put+remove+marks in one transaction, marks by prefix), the save batch of binary frames (transferred to the writer), gzip of the frames 32 at a time, the writer in the job system's save lane (portWriter) or in place, and the database 'roadcraft-saves'.
- src/editor/smallCityMap.ts: A whole city made on one document in a worker: planned (small or large plan), laid out (footways, network, lots, green, zones), every lot grown on flat ground; either written out whole as JSON (buildCityMap) or with its buildings spilled into a table as they grow (buildCityTable, the page's PreparedCity), or laid out only for the tile growth (layoutCityOf).
- src/editor/snap.ts: The road tool's snapping: what the pointer is over (a node, a point on a road, open ground) found among the ribbons near the point; road starts and ends snapped to nodes, roads (with perpendicular/angle landings), headings (continue, perpendicular, grid orthogonals, 15-degree steps), lengths and the grid; heights of anchors; the snap settings kept in localStorage.
- src/editor/squaresDecode.ts: A map saved by squares decoded from its blobs off the page (squaresRead.worker): globals and wears read, every square decompressed 32 at a time, tables of the kinds kept in tables kept per square (copied into shared memory) and ordered by id (not joined), the rest decoded as records, lists ordered; the layout for the next save; timings.
- src/editor/squaresRead.worker.ts: The job-system service that decodes a saved map's squares off the page (decodeSquares) and posts the session back: plain table buffers, orders, wear fields and upgraded frames transferred; shared tables shared; the document's records (roads, lists such as trees, items not in tables) structured-cloned.
- src/editor/storeTurns.ts: The save worker's turns: writes run at once; reads run one at a time, each begun in a task of its own (a MessageChannel message to itself) once no write is under way, so a write never waits behind queued synchronous reads.
- src/editor/streetObjects.ts: Street objects that split a road: a pedestrian crossing (zebra or signal) or a U-turn through the median at a chosen arc position, each made on a forked document and network, checked (the crossing's single crosswalk, the U-turn's connector fitting a car), and taken back.
- src/editor/streetscapeTool.ts: The landscaping tool: benches, bins, lights, trees and signs placed on the footways (snapped, refused with a reason), a stop or give-way sign at an approach becoming that leg's rule, shift-click removing, and a row dragged out from a placed item at the catalogue's spacing.
- src/editor/terrainBrush.ts: The terrain brush: what a dab does for every brush of the land tool (elements laid or erased, trees planted or cut, gully, fog and paint dabs, terrain stamps for raise/lower/flatten/river/landforms), and the stroke that lays dabs along the pointer at a spacing and a rate set by the last rebuild's cost, a held flatten repeating from the frame.
- src/editor/tileCodec.ts: Reading back the oldest tiled saves (manifest 5: gzip JSON squares) and the wears kept beside every format's buildings: a square's records, a wears list as id/decay pairs, and a record with its stored wear laid over it.
- src/editor/toolMachine.ts: The tool in hand as a statechart: every tool a state under 'hand' (the road tools under 'roads'); pick takes a tool, toggle takes it or puts it down to inspect, putDown returns to inspect and lets go of what Inspect picked; entering a tool tells the hands.
