## What changed
- measure.ts (the solids that hide, after "The convex solids that can hide"): a convex component hides only when CLOSED (every edge, by
  welded corner ids, shared by exactly two of its triangles) or when ALL its open edges lie on ONE plane (within eps 1e-4), any orientation;
  that plane is then one of its faces. An edge used three times or more: it hides nothing. An open curved strip hides nothing.
- Every face plane turned so the solid's inside is negative; inside = under every plane by more than eps ("Strictly inside"): a triangle
  lying on a face, the open plane too, is not inside.
- The bounds test with the same eps (`cx <= s.min[0]! + eps || ...`; it had none).
- Face to face pairs unchanged: the second triangle of a pair counts, as before.
- The criterion's text: model-catalog.mjs `HIDDEN` (its only change), and measure.ts's two comments.

## Tests (tests/tooling/catalogHidden.spec.ts, new)
1 an open strip arching over a triangle hides none; 2 a closed box hides the triangle inside; 3 a box open along its bottom hides the
triangle inside; 4 a triangle lying on that open plane is not counted; 5 a pair pressed face to face counts one (the second, as always).
