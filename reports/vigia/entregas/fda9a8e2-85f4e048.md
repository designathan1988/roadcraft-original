## What changed
- The lines along a road (centre line single, double or broken, lane dividers, bus lane edges) are data, not strokes:
  `segmentAlongPaint` (src/world/markings.ts:98) gives offset, width, colour, broken or not, the painted stretch [from, to]
  between the stop lines and the dash frame. `segmentMarkings` (src/world/markings.ts:159) now gives only turn arrows and bus
  legends. `roadAlongPaint` (src/render/paintQuads.ts:182) cuts at the stop lines/crossings as `roadStrokes` did.
- Paint table (src/render/roadPaint.ts:29): two float DataTextures read with texelFetch. Roads' table: 2 texels per road at its
  document's base + its id (`reserve` :41, `set` :56). Profiles' table: one row per distinct set of lines.
- Collision fix: each document gets its own block (`reserve`, src/render/roadPaint.ts:41), so documents with the same ids (the
  renderer's warm-up roads, the lab's layouts) do not overwrite each other. The base is reserved per build
  (src/render/roadSurfaces.ts:371) and enters every tile's digest (src/render/roadSurfaces.ts:431), so a stored tile from a session
  where the document had another base is rebuilt, never drawn with wrong slots.
- Each road's look writes its row (src/render/roadSurfacePass.ts:46). The carriageway's `roadSection` is now (across, along, slot)
  (src/render/roadSurfaceReads.ts:198), -1 off any road's span (plates). A triangle whose corners read two roads is re-read in the
  frame of the road nearest its centroid (`asphaltSectionFrame` src/render/roadSurfaceReads.ts:207, src/render/mesh/tileMesh.ts:133).
- Shader (src/render/mesh/roadEdges.ts:18 `CARRIAGEWAY_PAINT`, uniforms :74): each line's box-filtered coverage of the pixel
  across and along, the broken ones by the integral of the dash pattern, worn as `markings.ts` paintMaterial wore it; roughness
  0.72 under paint; the kerb-side dirt and wheel tracks read the road's half width and lane width from the table. The section's
  third number is a flat varying.
- Off-road sentinel unified to z = -1 (tileMesh.ts, surfaceTiles.ts, roadProgramStandIns.ts, footway section in roadSurfaceReads.ts).
- Sheets draw the carriageway with its real paint shader (src/lab/catalog/items.ts:117) so the lines can be photographed.

## Technique
- Analytic, box-filtered procedural markings in the surface shader (no polygon, no draw, no overdraw): Quilez, filterable
  procedurals, https://iquilezles.org/articles/filterableprocedurals ; dash pattern integral as in Rougier, JCGT 2(2) 2013,
  https://jcgt.org/published/0002/02/08/ . Choice from reports/Vias e mobiliário urbano otimizados.md (markings section).
