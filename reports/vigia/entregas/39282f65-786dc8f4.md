## What changed
- measure.ts:274-330 (the solids that hide): a convex component hides only when CLOSED (every edge, by welded corner ids, shared by exactly
  two of its triangles) or when ALL its open edges lie on ONE plane (within the same eps, 1e-4), any orientation; that plane is then added
  as one of its faces. A component with an edge used three times or more hides nothing; an open curved strip hides nothing.
- Every face plane is turned so the solid's inside is negative; "inside" = under every plane by more than eps (measure.ts, the loop after
  "Strictly inside"): a triangle lying on a face, the open plane too, is not inside.
- The bounds test has the same eps (it had none): measure.ts, the line `cx <= s.min[0]! + eps || ...`.
- Face to face pairs unchanged (the second triangle of a pair counts, as before).
- The criterion's text: scripts/model-catalog.mjs `HIDDEN`, and measure.ts's two comments, say the same rule.

## Tests (tests/tooling/catalogHidden.spec.ts, new)
1. an open strip arching over a triangle hides none; 2. a closed box hides the triangle inside; 3. a box open along its bottom hides the
triangle inside; 4. a triangle lying on that open plane is not counted; 5. a pair pressed face to face still counts (one, as the code
always counted it: the second of the pair).
The same spec run against the OLD measure.ts (worktree at 759af93f): case 1 fails (the defect), 2-5 pass. Against the new one: 5 of 5 pass.
