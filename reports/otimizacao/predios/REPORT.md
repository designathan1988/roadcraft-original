# Procedural buildings: research, diagnosis, first rebuild step

## How studios build many buildings (sources)

- Buildings are a parametric description (footprint, storeys, bays, style, seed) turned into **instances of a kit plus a facade shader**, not unique meshes. Matrix Awakens / City Sample: ~7,000 buildings from ~2,000 kit meshes, ~7 M instances, generated by Houdini rules ([Epic docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-quick-start-for-generating-a-city-and-freeway-using-houdini), [CGW](https://www.cgw.com/Press-Center/In-Focus/2021/The-Matrix-Awaits-in-The-Matrix-Awakens-An-Unrea.aspx), [cgchannel](https://www.cgchannel.com/2022/04/download-epic-games-free-city-sample-assets-for-ue5)).
- The generator is a split grammar: mass, faces, floors, bays, terminal modules ([Wonka, SIGGRAPH 2011 course](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/cgvlab/urban/sg_2011_course/umc_SG11_03_computational_building_design.pdf), [CityEngine CGA](https://doc.arcgis.com/en/cityengine/latest/tutorials/tutorial-9-advanced-shape-grammar.htm)).
- Windows and interiors are drawn in the shader: interior mapping ([van Dongen](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/interior-mapping-rendering-real-rooms-without-geometry); SimCity 2013, City Sample `M_Window` ([Versluis](https://versluis.com/2024/03/how-to-dim-the-window-lights-in-epics-city-sample-project))). Night windows come from a per-window hash.
- LOD is chosen by screen-space error, with merged proxies far away ([Cesium](https://community.cesium.com/t/announcing-cesium-osm-buildings/9757), [UE HLOD](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/world-partition---hierarchical-level-of-detail-in-unreal-engine)). Even Nanite needs merged proxies for distant instances ([Nanite, SIGGRAPH 2021](https://advances.realtimerendering.com/s2021/Karis_Nanite_SIGGRAPH_Advances_2021_final.pdf)).
- Cities: Skylines II is the counter-example: props without LODs, every object casting shadows (72 % of draws), no occlusion culling ([Huhtala](https://blog.paavo.me/cities-skylines-2-performance/)).
- WebGL2/three.js limits: no indirect draw or compute; BatchedMesh slows past ~100 k items ([three.js #28776](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/28776)).

## How the game does it today

- **Data (src/world/buildings): sound.** Volumes → storeys → bays → components with a style grammar (`architecture.ts`) and a seeded made-to-measure generator (`procedural.ts`). It is a CGA-like split grammar: the same family as the sources above.
- **Rendering (src/render/buildings): the right family, with structural faults.**
  - The right family: a kit drawn instanced with screen-error levels, facade windows by shader with a real per-pane interior box, screen-error HLOD, workers.
  - The faults:
    - The same building is emitted five times, once per level (`emitChunk` near and far, `emitMassing` ×3, `emitBase`). An edit rebuilds its whole 2 km plate.
    - At the detail level every bay was laid as its own wall faces.
    - Quantization is undone on the GPU (`shellBatch.ts` back to Float32; `gpuBatchedMesh.ts` 32-bit indices).
    - The kit's near models carry most of the triangles.

## Step 1 done: the detail level's facade on runs (commit 6a2ba7be)

- A plain wall bay, or one around an opening its own pane closes, is now laid by its storey's run: one quad per run of alike bays, drawn by the facade shader over the near shell (`kit.facadeNear`, `FACADE_KIND.covered`). The wall's rain streak is drawn by the shader too.
- The wall's tone is computed per pixel.
- Panes (interior box, recess, frame), sills, props and cut openings (doors, roller shutters, porches) are unchanged.
- Ruins get the runs back as plain faces with their tone.
